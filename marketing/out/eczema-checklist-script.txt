Shopping for someone with eczema? Here's a five-step checklist.
One: ask for their list. If they've been patch tested, ask for the results sheet or their avoid list.
Two: read the label, not the front. "Gentle" and "hypoallergenic" are not guarantees. The ingredient list is.
Three: if fragrance is on their list, skip anything that lists "fragrance" or "parfum."
Four: in the store, scan the barcode, then check the product against their avoid list.
Five: when unsure, ask their dermatologist or a pharmacist. Facial swelling, blistering, trouble breathing, or a spreading rash with fever needs urgent care.
